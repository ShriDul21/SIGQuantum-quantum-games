A tiny refresher +
X

Flips the bit.
X|0⟩ = |1⟩

Y

Flips, with a phase.
Y|0⟩ = i|1⟩

Z

Flips the phase.
Z|1⟩ = −|1⟩

H

Makes superposition.
H|0⟩ = |+⟩

|+⟩ = (|0⟩ + |1⟩)/√2   ·   |−⟩ = (|0⟩ − |1⟩)/√2
We keep exact phases here, even when they’re physically equivalent as global phases.
